Terror on the Midway (1942)

Famous Studios · Superman

Premieres on the channel Sat Nov 7.

Why it’s public domain

Published 1942 (the title card carries a 1942 Paramount Pictures copyright notice). We found no renewal in the Catalog of Copyright Entries renewal volumes for 1969 to 1971 (the 28th-year window). Under the 1909 Act, an unrenewed copyright expired after 28 years.

Evidence: Strong. More than one independent sign points the same way (publication date, a published list, and our own renewal search).

Our print

An older Technicolor film transfer with the original "A Max Fleischer Cartoon / Superman in Technicolor" opening, the title card with its Paramount copyright line, and the full credit card. It is soft and faded in places, and we have not cleaned, re-scored or restored it.

https://archive.org/details/superman-terror-on-the-midway

We passed on a 4K upscale and on a 1080p copy that looked like a modern remaster.

What isn’t free

The film is free, but Superman's name, costume and logo are DC Comics trademarks, and the "Superman March" composition may still draw automated music matches. We never use the character as our branding.
