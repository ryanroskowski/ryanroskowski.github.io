How a Mosquito Operates (1912)

Winsor McCay · Curiosities

Premieres on the channel Tue Nov 3.

Why it’s public domain

A US film first shown in 1912 and released in 1916, so its US copyright term has long expired (everything published before 1931 is public domain in the US).

Evidence: Strong. It was published before 1931, so its US term has simply run out and no renewal search is needed.

Our print

A scratched, dusty black-and-white film transfer, shown silent as made (there are no intertitles and no soundtrack, and we have not added a modern score). The explosion was hand-painted red on McCay's own prints, and those colored versions don't survive. We trimmed a closing "The End" title from this copy; otherwise it is uncleaned and unrestored. A live-action prologue with McCay is lost.

https://archive.org/details/WinsorMcCay1912HowAMosquitoOperates

We passed on a copy with a modern digital title card and one with a modern electronic score added.
