The Cobweb Hotel (1936)

Fleischer Studios · Color Classics

Premieres on the channel Thu Oct 29.

Why it’s public domain

Published 1936. We found no renewal in the Catalog of Copyright Entries, Motion Pictures renewal volumes for 1963 and 1964 (the 28th-year window). Under the 1909 Act, an unrenewed copyright expired after 28 years.

The only Paramount one-reel renewals in the 1963 and 1964 volumes are 1935–37 Popeye shorts, so the silence on this title isn't an artifact of a missing studio.

Evidence: Strong. More than one independent sign points the same way (publication date, a published list, and our own renewal search).

Our print

An older 16mm Technicolor print with its original Paramount opening and closing titles (from the Bill Sprague Collection on the Internet Archive). It is faded and scratched, and we have not cleaned, re-scored or restored it.

https://archive.org/details/BillSpragueCollectionTheCobwebHotel

What isn’t free

The film is free, but Paramount and Fleischer logos and any recurring characters stay out of our own branding.
