The Goose That Laid the Golden Egg (1936)

Van Beuren · Rainbow Parade

Premieres on the channel Thu Oct 22.

Why it’s public domain

Listed in Wikipedia 'List of animated films in the public domain in the United States' (copyright not renewed).

RKO, the series' distributor, did renew its 1935–36 feature films in 1963 and 1964 (including Van Beuren's own Frank Buck's Fang and Claw), but renewed none of the one-reel cartoons, and no other Rainbow Parade title appears either.

Evidence: Good. A published list plus our own renewal search, with no contrary evidence.
