Van Beuren Studios · Aesop's Sound Fables

Premieres on the channel Tue Oct 27.

Why it’s public domain

Published in 1930 with notice ("Copyright 1930" on the title card). The US copyright term has expired, and the film entered the US public domain on 1 January 2026.

Evidence: Strong. It was published before 1931, so its US term has simply run out and no renewal search is needed.

Our print

An older black-and-white transfer with the original Pathé "Aesop's Sound Fables" titles and the Van Beuren "The End" card. We added only the opening context card and raised the overall volume. We have not cleaned, re-scored or restored it.
