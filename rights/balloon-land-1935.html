Balloon Land (1935)

Ub Iwerks · ComiColor Cartoons

Premieres on the channel Thu Oct 29.

Why it’s public domain

Published 1935. We found no renewal in the Catalog of Copyright Entries renewal volumes for 1962 and 1963 (the 28th-year window). Under the 1909 Act, an unrenewed copyright expired after 28 years.

The same distributor did renew a few earlier ComiColor shorts (Jack and the Beanstalk, The Little Red Hen and The Valiant Tailor), so we checked this title by name rather than assuming the whole series was free.

Evidence: Strong. More than one independent sign points the same way (publication date, a published list, and our own renewal search).

Our print

An older film transfer with the original P. A. Powers / ComiColor opening, the "Copyright MCMXXXV by Celebrity Productions" credit and the "Finis" end card. It is soft and grainy, and we have not cleaned, re-scored or restored it.

https://archive.org/details/comi-color-cartoons-1935x-08-balloon-land

We passed on a copy labeled "restored" and on a smaller 320×240 copy of the same print.

What isn’t free

We never use studio logos or recurring characters as Jolly Good Show branding.
