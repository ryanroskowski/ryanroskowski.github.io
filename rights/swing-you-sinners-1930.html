Swing You Sinners! (1930)

Fleischer Studios · Talkartoons

Premieres on the channel Wed Oct 28.

Why it’s public domain

Published in 1930. The US copyright term has expired, and the film entered the US public domain on 1 January 2026.

Evidence: Strong. It was published before 1931, so its US term has simply run out and no renewal search is needed.

Our print

A 1950s U.M.&M. TV Corp. television print, which replaced the original Paramount opening and closing titles with U.M.&M. cards. We have not cleaned, re-scored or restored it.

https://archive.org/details/SwingYouSinners

We passed on a hand-colorized copy.

What isn’t free

The film is free, but Paramount and Fleischer logos and any recurring characters stay out of our own branding.
