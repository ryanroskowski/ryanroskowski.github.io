The Bulleteers (1942)

Fleischer Studios · Superman

Premieres on the channel Sat Oct 31.

Why it’s public domain

Published 1942 (title card: Copyright MCMXLII by Paramount Pictures Inc.). We found no renewal in the Catalog of Copyright Entries renewal volumes for 1969 to 1971 (the 28th-year window); "Superman" has no hits in any 1962 to 1971 volume. Under the 1909 Act, an unrenewed copyright expired after 28 years.

Evidence: Strong. More than one independent sign points the same way (publication date, a published list, and our own renewal search).

Our print

An older Technicolor film transfer with its original Paramount mountain logo, the "Superman in Technicolor" opening and the full credit card, and the Paramount logo at the end. It is faded and dark in places, and we have not cleaned, re-scored or restored it.

https://archive.org/details/bulleteers

What isn’t free

The film is free, but Superman's name, costume and logo are DC Comics trademarks, and the "Superman March" composition may still draw automated music matches. We never use the character as our branding.
