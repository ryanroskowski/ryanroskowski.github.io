The Haunted Mouse (1941)

Leon Schlesinger Productions · Looney Tunes

Premieres on the channel Fri Oct 30.

Why it’s public domain

Published 1941 with a copyright notice. We found no renewal in the Catalog of Copyright Entries renewal volumes for 1967 to 1970 (the 28th-year window was 1968–69). Under the 1909 Act, an unrenewed copyright expired after 28 years.

Evidence: Strong. More than one independent sign points the same way (publication date, a published list, and our own renewal search).

Our print

A 1950s television print. In 1955 Warner Bros. licensed its black-and-white cartoons to Guild Films through a subsidiary, Sunset Productions, and those TV prints open with a "Copyright MXMXLI by Sunset Productions, Inc." card (the Roman numerals really are wrong on these cards) and close with a "The End / A Guild Films Presentation" card in place of the Warner ending. In between is the complete cartoon with its original Looney Tunes opening, title card and Carl Stalling soundtrack. It is a soft standard-definition transfer that has been enlarged to 1440×1080, and we have not cleaned, re-scored or restored it.

https://archive.org/details/looney-tunes-sunset-productions-1930-to-1943_202607 (file "160-The Haunted Mouse")

What isn’t free

The film is free, but the Looney Tunes and Merrie Melodies names, logos and characters are Warner Bros. trademarks, so we never use them as our branding.
