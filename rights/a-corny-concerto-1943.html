A Corny Concerto (1943)

Warner Bros. (Clampett) · Merrie Melodies

Premieres on the channel Tue Oct 27.

Why it’s public domain

Listed in Wikipedia 'List of animated films in the public domain in the United States' (copyright not renewed); also listed in an Internet Archive collection of the Looney Tunes and Merrie Melodies that their owner at renewal time (United Artists, through a.a.p.) failed to renew. No renewal found in the Catalog of Copyright Entries (1970–1971 renewal volumes).

Evidence: Strong. More than one independent sign points the same way (publication date, a published list, and our own renewal search).

Our print

https://archive.org/details/acornyconcerto1943_201909

What isn’t free

The film is free, but the Looney Tunes and Merrie Melodies names, logos and characters are Warner Bros. trademarks, so we never use them as our branding.
