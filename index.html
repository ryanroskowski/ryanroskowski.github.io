What’s on

Jolly Good Show plays American cartoons from the 1930s and ’40s that are now in the public domain, plus strange old whimsies from the same archives: Technicolor dreamscapes, odd little fables, fairy tales, wartime shorts, trick films, novelty reels and vintage puppets, shown as audiences first saw them.

Every video lists its year, studio, archive source and the reason it is in the public domain. When a film reflects ugly attitudes of its era, we say so plainly, and we leave out shorts built around racist caricature.

Want the receipts? The Rights Ledger shows the evidence for every film on the channel: the renewal records we searched, where each print came from, and what stays protected.
