Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This policy covers the Google OAuth application registered as “Rubberhose Radio” / Jolly Good Show (the “App”), operated by Ryan Roskowski.

What the App is

The App is a private, single-user tool. Only the owner of the Jolly Good Show YouTube channel signs in to it. It is not offered to the public, and no other person can sign in.

What it does with Google data

The App uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the owner’s own channel and to edit that channel’s settings, such as its description and keywords. It does not access any other user’s account or data.

Storage of credentials

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner’s own server. They are never shared with anyone else and are used only to make the API calls described above.

Viewers and the public

The App collects no data from viewers or website visitors. This website uses no analytics or tracking cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with anyone.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The App’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube and Google

By using YouTube features through the App, you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google’s handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

You can revoke the App’s access to your Google account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
